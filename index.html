UNCOMPROMISING STANDARDS

Exquisite Materials &
Master Construction Specs

Built with precision aluminium formwork engineering and adorned with high-grade imported finishes built to endure across generations.

Civil & Wall Engineering

  • Earthquake-resistant RCC structure built with Aluminium Formwork (Mivan technology).
  • Floor-to-ceiling clear volume of 3.35 meters (11 feet) across all living rooms & suites.
  • Internal walls finished with premium Gypsum/Neeru coat and washable plastic emulsion paint.
  • All-weather weather-shield exterior texture paint engineered for monsoon durability.

Flooring & Verandahs

  • Living, Dining, Foyer, Kitchen & Bedrooms finished with Italian Marble Flooring.
  • Balconies and private sky decks paved with rustic wooden finish anti-skid vitrified tiles.
  • Full-height designer wall dado tiles up to 8 feet in all luxury bathrooms.
  • Granite/Composite marble kitchen platform with double-bowl SS sink.

Security & Home Automation

  • Biometric digital smart security door lock (Fingerprint, Passcode & RFID access).
  • Heavy-duty powder-coated sound-insulating aluminium windows with SGU acoustic glass.
  • Concealed copper wiring with FRLS fire-retardant cables & premium modular Schneider/Legrand switches.
  • Video door phone integrated with main security gatehouse concierge.

Safety & Sustainability

  • 24x7 High-definition CCTV network covering all campus access gates, podiums, and lobbies.
  • Dedicated Emergency Response Room & 24x7 Nurse Quarters stationed on the 2nd floor.
  • High-speed passenger and stretcher elevators with 100% generator power backup.
  • Eco-conscious rainwater harvesting, organic waste converter (OWC), and sewage treatment plant (STP).
